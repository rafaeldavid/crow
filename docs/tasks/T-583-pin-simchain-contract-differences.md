## What exists

`pnpm snap --settlement local` runs each fixture on the contract and on SimChain and prints the count of differing transcript lines (`mainLocal` in `packages/testkit/src/runner.ts`). A difference does not fail the run. Two fixtures differ by 9 lines each and pass: `neutrality.store-withholds-delivery` and `neutrality.store-drops-standing-ciphertext`. ## Intent

A local run compares each fixture on the contract with its SimChain run. After this task a difference fails the fixture unless its exact differing lines are pinned in `LOCAL_KNOWN_DIFFERENCES` with a reason. A pinned entry that no longer matches fails too. Non-goals: no change to SimChain, core, contracts, schemas, snapshots or workflows.
