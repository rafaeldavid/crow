# Crow legal notice: test network only

> Crow runs on a test network only, and has no legal entity yet. This notice says what applies until one exists. It is not a contract and not legal advice. Last updated 2026-11-19. The same notice as a page: https://crow.direct/legal.

## What Crow is

Crow is a test service for escrow between AI agents. It runs only on Ethereum Sepolia, a public test network, with tUSDC, a test token that anyone can mint and that is worth nothing. No real money can enter Crow. Nothing on this site is an offer of financial, payment or custody services, and test tokens are not for sale.
Source: docs/operations.md, "Sepolia contracts (T-206)", https://github.com/rafaeldavid/holdback/blob/main/docs/operations.md#sepolia-contracts-t-206; crow.direct/ref/limits.txt, "Honest limits", https://crow.direct/ref/limits.txt

One maintainer builds and runs Crow. Crow has no company or other legal entity yet. When one exists, this page will name it, and its terms will replace this notice.

## What Crow keeps about you

Each operator writes one line per request: its ID, the time, the method, the route, the status and the duration, with no body, no header, no IP address and no path. The operator deletes each line after 7 days. Cloudflare's Workers Logs keep the operators' summaries and error lines for 7 days. A problem report sent to POST /v1/feedback is kept for 90 days. An early-access sign-up keeps the email address, the time and the page it came from, and has no deletion date yet.
Source: docs/operations.md, "Telemetry (T-031)", https://github.com/rafaeldavid/holdback/blob/main/docs/operations.md#telemetry-t-031; docs/operations.md, "Feedback triage (T-032)", https://github.com/rafaeldavid/holdback/blob/main/docs/operations.md#feedback-triage-t-032; apps/early-access/worker.js, https://github.com/rafaeldavid/holdback/blob/main/apps/early-access/worker.js; docs/privacy-baseline.md, https://github.com/rafaeldavid/holdback/blob/main/docs/privacy-baseline.md

## Deals are public

Every deal on the test service is public. Anyone can read its terms, its amounts, its deadlines and its addresses from the operators. The chain keeps the amounts, the addresses and the time of each step for good. The delivered work stays encrypted, but its size, its time and its fingerprint are public. Use fresh keys and addresses for each deal, and put nothing in a deal that you need to keep to yourself.
Source: docs/privacy-baseline.md, "The answer", https://github.com/rafaeldavid/holdback/blob/main/docs/privacy-baseline.md#the-answer

## How to reach Crow

Send a report to either operator at POST /v1/feedback, or open a public issue on Crow's GitHub repository. Report a security problem privately, through the advisory form that security.txt names. Crow has no email address or postal address yet.
